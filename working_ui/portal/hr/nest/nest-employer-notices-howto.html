NEST - informing workers (employer notices)

How Sevitha / HR should use NEST Employer notices when auto-enrolling staff. Workplace scheme: NEST (nestpensions.org.uk).

What are the Word / PDF templates?

NEST gives employers two kinds of download:

  1. Guides (PDF) - e.g. Telling your staff about their new pension scheme. Explains what to send and when.
  2. Letter templates (usually Word .docx) - editable letters you personalise and send to each worker (email or post to their personal address). These are the actual statutory notices.
We do not invent these letters. Download them from the NEST employer account / NEST communications toolkit, fill in clubSENsational details, and send them. Keep a filled master copy in: docs/hr/nest/templates/.

Where to download

How this fits with our employment contracts

DocumentWhenPurpose
Employment contract + Annex A (NEST) When issuing / signing the contract General workplace pension info (already in the portal generator)
NEST employer notice (letter template) When assessing / auto-enrolling (or postponement / opt-in) Statutory notice required by Pensions Act 2008 - personalised to that worker
Annex A does not replace the NEST enrolment notice. Both are needed for a clean compliance trail.

Sevitha checklist (every enrolment)

  1. Confirm worker category for this pay period (eligible jobholder / can opt in / can join).
  2. Enrol in NEST (or note postponement / opt-in request) via payroll / NEST.
  3. Pick the matching NEST letter template for that category.
  4. Personalise: worker name, clubSENsational Ltd, scheme = NEST, contribution rates, HR contact, enrolment / postponement dates.
  5. Send to the worker's personal email or postal address (not only WhatsApp).
  6. Save PDF/email proof in the worker's HR file.
  7. Deadline: usually within 6 weeks of the relevant date (start / enrolment / duties start - follow the NEST guide for the exact trigger).

First-time scheme setup vs ongoing new starters

  • Just finished NEST setup: use the "setting up for the first time" guide + send notices to current workers who need them.
  • Later new starters: use the "enrolling a new worker" guide each time someone becomes eligible or is enrolled.
  • Every 3 years: re-enrolment cycle - separate NEST guide/templates.
