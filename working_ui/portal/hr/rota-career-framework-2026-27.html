8. Day Centre Career Framework

Three progression bands (A / B / C) plus a separate Service Lead role. English text for contracts / HR use.

Philosophy

The Day Centre Career Framework has been designed to recognise professional development, leadership, accountability and service quality.

Salary progression is based on increasing responsibility, performance and contribution to the organisation, rather than length of service alone.

Progression is never automatic and is reviewed through the annual Performance Review process.

Length of service alone is not a criterion for promotion or a salary increase. Tenure may be considered as an additional factor, but promotion depends on demonstrated ability to perform the responsibilities of the next level.

Contract Structure

All Day Centre employees will be employed under a:

  • Permanent Part-Time (Term-Time Only) contract.
  • Guaranteed weekly hours.
  • Annual salary paid equally over 12 months.
  • Salary linked to Career Band rather than individual negotiation.

Career structure (confirmed)

Day Centre progression has three career bands (A → B → C). Above that sits a separate Service Lead role (not a fourth band).

Band A — Day Centre Support Worker

The entry / standard Day Centre support role. Provides high-quality person-centred support while following clubSENsational procedures and standards.

Typical responsibilities

  • Deliver individualised participant support.
  • Follow behaviour support plans.
  • Maintain safeguarding standards.
  • Complete session records accurately.
  • Communicate professionally with families and colleagues.
  • Maintain organisation and professionalism.
  • Work collaboratively within the team.

Expected behaviour

Reliable · Professional · Safe practice · Consistent · Positive team member

£18.00 / hour £15,480 annual (20h / 43 weeks)

Entry point for all new Day Centre staff.

Band B — Experienced Day Centre Support Worker

Mid progression between Support Worker and Senior. More autonomy and consistency than Band A, without full senior ownership of the service.

Additional responsibilities

  • Work with greater independence on familiar participants.
  • Support newer Band A colleagues in daily practice.
  • Handle routine behaviour and session decisions with confidence.
  • Maintain strong documentation and family communication.
  • Contribute ideas for small service improvements.
  • Model reliable, professional standards.

Expected behaviour

Dependable · Growing autonomy · Clear communication · Steady quality · Team support

£20.00 / hour £17,200 annual (20h / 43 weeks)

Mid step between Band A and Senior (Band C).
2026–27 contracts: Roberto · Yousef · Luliya (si trabajan).

Band C — Senior Day Centre Support Worker

The senior support progression within the Day Centre. Greater ownership of participants, daily organisation and service quality. Mentors Bands A and B.

Additional responsibilities

  • Mentor new staff and Band A / B colleagues.
  • Lead activities independently.
  • Support behaviour management.
  • Assist with staff induction.
  • Maintain excellent documentation.
  • Identify service improvements.
  • Model best practice.
  • Build positive relationships with families.

Expected behaviour

Highly autonomous · Solution focused · Role model · Strong communication · Consistently high standards

£24.00 / hour £20,640 annual (20h / 43 weeks)

Senior progression band. Not used for Michelle (she moves to Service Lead role).

Day Centre Service Lead — separate role (not a career band)

A distinct management post, outside the A → B → C progression. Appointment is by role / vacancy, not automatic promotion from Band C.

Role focus

  • Lead the daily operation of the Day Centre.
  • Coordinate staff and allocate resources.
  • Coach and mentor the team (Bands A–C).
  • Support complex behaviours and safeguarding decisions.
  • Review participant programmes and documentation quality.
  • Contribute to recruitment, training and service development.
  • Act as the accountable senior when directors are unavailable.

Expected behaviour

Leadership · Excellent judgement · High accountability · Decision making · Coaching others · Service improvement

£30.00 / hour £25,800 annual (20h / 43 weeks)

Not a fourth band. Separate contract / role title when appointed.
2026–27 contract: Michelle · £30/h · ~20h.

Progression Principles

Promotion is based on demonstrated competence, not length of service.

Within the Day Centre career path, employees may:

  • Remain on Band A (£18/h) — Support Worker.
  • Progress to Band B (£20/h) — Experienced Support Worker.
  • Progress to Band C (£24/h) — Senior Support Worker.
  • Apply for / be appointed to the separate Service Lead role (£30/h) when that post exists — not an automatic fourth band.

Each step requires a successful Performance Review (or formal appointment for Service Lead).

Annual Performance Review Criteria

  • Quality of participant support
  • Safeguarding compliance
  • Attendance and reliability
  • Professional conduct
  • Documentation quality
  • Communication
  • Initiative
  • Problem solving
  • Leadership
  • Team contribution
  • Service development
  • Family and stakeholder feedback

Salary Review Philosophy

Annual salary reviews should consider:

  • Individual performance
  • Increased responsibilities
  • Completion of internal training
  • Business growth
  • Financial sustainability
  • Market benchmarking

Salary progression is not guaranteed and remains subject to business performance and the outcome of the annual review.
