Counting rule
Last week of each term: always count as 32 even if actual hours are lower — the week ends Friday, so the usual Sunday (~6.5 h) is missing by design, not as a shortfall. Weeks where he does work Sunday should hit 32: less = he owes the club; more = the club owes him.
