clubSENsational

What we offer

clubSENsational provides specialist activity sessions and daytime support for children with autism and learning disabilities. Our team delivers structured, sensory-aware sessions across trusted venues - helping each child build confidence, skills, and independence.

Aquatic Activity

Acton / Northolt / SwimFarm

1:1 swimming with sensory-aware instructors - water confidence, regulation, and independence in the pool. Sessions are paced to each child so they feel safe, supported, and ready to progress.

Climbing Activity

Westway

Supported climbing that builds strength, focus, and confidence. Routes and support levels are matched to each child so they can challenge themselves safely.

Physical Activity

SwimFarm / Acton

Active movement sessions for coordination, stamina, and structure - adapted so every child can take part safely and stay engaged throughout the session.

Multi-Activity

SwimFarm / Northolt

A 90-minute activity that combines swimming with other classroom activities - ideal for children who benefit from variety and a longer, structured session in one visit.

Bespoke Programme

SwimFarm / Acton / Westway

A tailored programme built around your child's goals, support needs, and schedule - planned with you so the support stays consistent and meaningful.

Day Centre

SwimFarm

Weekday daytime support with structured activity, life skills, swimming, and community trips when staffing allows - a full daytime rhythm with familiar staff.

Holiday crash courses

School holidays

Short intensive weeks in the holidays - climbing and swimming crash courses when dates are open for booking. A focused burst of activity while school is closed.
