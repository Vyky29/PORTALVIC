ClubSEN / clubSENsational

Our activities

Specialist sessions for children and young people with autism and learning disabilities.

Aquatic Activity

From 3 years / Acton / Northolt / SwimFarm

1:1 or small-group swimming with sensory-aware instructors - water confidence, regulation, and independence in the pool.

Climbing Activity

From 3 years / Westway

Supported climbing that builds strength, focus, and confidence, with routes matched to each child.

Physical Activity

From 12 years / SwimFarm / Acton

Active movement sessions for coordination, stamina, and structure - adapted so every young person can take part safely.

Multi-Activity

From 3 years / SwimFarm / Northolt

A longer visit that combines activities in one block - ideal when you want more variety in a single session.

Bespoke Programme

From 3 years / SwimFarm / Acton / Westway

A tailored programme built around your child's goals, support needs, and schedule.

Day Centre

Mon-Fri / 11am-4pm / SwimFarm

Weekday daytime support with structured activity, life skills, swimming, and community trips when staffing allows.

Holiday crash courses

School holidays / Climbing at Westway / Swimming at Acton

Short intensive weeks in the holidays - climbing and swimming crash courses when dates are open for booking.
