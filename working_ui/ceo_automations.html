English

On a clock

Times are London. Pay hold, trial offers, the 06:00 buffer and the 21:00 office push are on the saved clock. Staff feedback WhatsApp is on. The timesheet reminder and the weekly notes are built for the times below.

WhatWhenWhat it does alone
Pay hold Every 2 minutes Around minute 25 of a 30-minute pay hold it nudges the parent by WhatsApp or email. When the 30 minutes end unpaid, the hold expires and the seat is free again.
After a paid trial Every 10 minutes When the trial session has ended, it sends the first offer. If the family has not moved by 20:00, it sends a second. At the end of the day it drops the soft hold and tells the office.
Own-arrangement buffer About 06:00 If a family on their own arrangement is below the prepaid buffer, it puts a soft hold. If the buffer is back, it clears that soft hold. It never cuts the sessions by itself.
Missing feedback, office 21:00 Push to admin about yesterday's missing session feedback.
Feedback WhatsApp to staff Sun-Fri 20:00, then 20:30. Saturday 18:00, then 18:30. WhatsApp to the worker who still owes feedback. The second message only goes if it is still missing. This chase is on.
Timesheet reminder 23:00 on the 24th WhatsApp to staff who have not submitted the timesheet for the cycle that ends that day. People outside payroll are skipped.
Weekly notes for parents Tuesday evening, and Saturday morning Writes the Saturday-to-Friday note into the family portal. No WhatsApp. Tuesday is for children who only come Saturday, Sunday or Monday. Saturday morning is for the rest of the week.

When something happens

No clock. The other system, or a saved change, starts it.

WhatStarts whenWhat it does alone
Stripe payment The parent pays Marks the invoice paid, can finish the booking, confirm a trial seat, tell the office, clear a payment hold, and put the payment in Xero.
GoCardless payment The bank payment or the mandate succeeds The booking can complete, the instalment is recorded, and a payment hold can clear.
GoCardless failed payment The bank payment fails Starts a grace window. A follow-up job is written to move an expired window onto Hold. The every-5-minutes clock for that follow-up is not in the saved schedule.
Schedule change or cover The change is saved Staff and leads get a push. The logo halo and the Quick menu tile light up. Parents do not see an instructor day off.
Outstanding feedback A session still needs feedback Halo and the Outstanding feedbacks tile. It does not lock the app with a sign-and-submit sheet.

Already finished

The date has passed. They do not keep acting on new days.

WhatWhenWhat it did
Unpaid Autumn release After 15 Aug 2026 Families whose first Autumn bank payment was still unpaid lost the seat, so Booking Portal could offer it. Live from 16 Aug 2026.
June payroll email 26 Jun 2026, morning One payroll email that morning. It does not repeat each month.

Still needs a person

None of these run alone: a new place, New client, a cover, a day off, creating an invoice, Xero products, an announcement, Family broadcast. The Office Operator does not write covers or invoices by itself.

Espanol

Con reloj

Las horas son de Londres. Pay hold, ofertas de trial, el buffer de las 06:00 y el push de las 21:00 a la oficina estan en el reloj guardado. El WhatsApp de feedback al staff esta encendido. El recordatorio de timesheet y las notas semanales estan hechos para las horas de abajo.

QueCuandoQue hace solo
Pay hold Cada 2 minutos Hacia el minuto 25 de un pay hold de 30 minutos avisa al padre por WhatsApp o email. Si los 30 minutos acaban sin pago, el hold caduca y la plaza queda libre.
Despues de un trial pagado Cada 10 minutos Cuando la sesion de trial ha terminado, manda la primera oferta. Si la familia no se ha movido a las 20:00, manda una segunda. Al final del dia suelta el soft hold y avisa a la oficina.
Buffer de own arrangement Sobre las 06:00 Si una familia en own arrangement esta por debajo del buffer pagado por adelantado, pone un soft hold. Si el buffer vuelve, quita ese soft hold. Nunca corta las sesiones solo.
Feedback que falta, oficina 21:00 Push al admin sobre el feedback de sesion que falto ayer.
WhatsApp de feedback al staff Dom-vie 20:00 y luego 20:30. Sabado 18:00 y luego 18:30. WhatsApp al trabajador que aun debe el feedback. El segundo mensaje solo sale si sigue faltando. Este aviso esta encendido.
Recordatorio de timesheet 23:00 el dia 24 WhatsApp a quien no ha enviado el timesheet del ciclo que acaba ese dia. Quien esta fuera de nomina no entra.
Notas semanales para padres Martes por la tarde, y sabado por la manana Escribe la nota de sabado a viernes en el portal de la familia. Sin WhatsApp. El martes es para ninos que solo vienen sabado, domingo o lunes. El sabado por la manana es para el resto de la semana.

Cuando pasa algo

Sin reloj. Lo arranca el otro sistema, o un cambio ya guardado.

QueEmpieza cuandoQue hace solo
Pago Stripe El padre paga Marca la factura pagada, puede cerrar la reserva, confirmar la plaza de trial, avisar a la oficina, quitar un payment hold y meter el pago en Xero.
Pago GoCardless El pago del banco o el mandato sale bien La reserva puede completarse, el plazo queda anotado y un payment hold puede quitarse.
GoCardless fallido El pago del banco falla Abre una ventana de gracia. Hay un trabajo escrito para pasar una ventana caducada a Hold. El reloj de cada 5 minutos de ese seguimiento no esta en el horario guardado.
Cambio de horario o cover El cambio se guarda Staff y leads reciben un push. El halo del logo y la ficha del Quick menu se encienden. Los padres no ven el day off de un instructor.
Feedback pendiente Una sesion sigue sin feedback Halo y la ficha Outstanding feedbacks. No bloquea la app con una hoja de firmar y enviar.

Ya terminado

La fecha ya paso. No siguen actuando en dias nuevos.
