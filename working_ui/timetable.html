Shorter blocks while settling in. Support is usually 1:1 on site (2:1 for pool transitions, personal care, or planned community trips). Places, hours, and funding are agreed with the office - not through online booking.

A typical day may include

  • Circle time and shared Hub activities with peers
  • Vocational tasks, maths, and handwriting
  • Gym circuits, basketball, and structured physical activity
  • Swimming / aquatic within the day
  • Lunch, life skills, and group snack
  • Sensory room and regulation time
  • Community trips when 2:1 cover is planned
