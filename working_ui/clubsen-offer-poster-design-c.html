What we offer

clubSENsational provides specialist activity sessions and daytime support for children with autism and learning disabilities. Our team delivers structured, sensory-aware sessions across trusted venues - helping each child build confidence, skills, and independence.

Aquatic Activity

Acton / Northolt / SwimFarm

1:1 swimming with sensory-aware instructors - water confidence, regulation, and independence in the pool.

Climbing Activity

Westway

Supported climbing that builds strength, focus, and confidence, with routes matched to each child.

Physical Activity

SwimFarm / Acton

Active movement sessions for coordination, stamina, and structure - adapted so every child can take part safely.

Multi-Activity

SwimFarm / Northolt

A 90-minute activity that combines swimming with other classroom activities - ideal for children who benefit from variety and a longer, structured session.

Bespoke Programme

SwimFarm / Acton / Westway

A tailored programme built around your child's goals, support needs, and schedule.

Day Centre

SwimFarm

Weekday daytime support with structured activity, life skills, swimming, and community trips when staffing allows.

Holiday crash courses

School holidays

Short intensive weeks in the holidays - climbing and swimming crash courses when dates are open for booking.
