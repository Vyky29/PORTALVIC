Services & prices · 2026/27

A simple guide to what we offer in the academic year 2026/27, where sessions run, and the standard parent-funded prices. Places are for children and young people with autism and/or learning disabilities, with 1:1 or 2:1 support depending on the place.

Prices below are the standard session rates used for re-enrolment 2026/27. Longer slots scale from the base unit (for example 60' Aquatic = 2 x 30'). VAT treatment is shown on your invoices / parent portal. LA or NHS funded places may differ and are confirmed individually.

Activity services

After-school and weekend sessions across our venues.

Aquatic Activity

£50 / 30'
From 3 years+ Usually 30' (also 60') Acton · Northolt · SwimFarm

1:1 or small-group swimming with sensory-aware instructors. We work at your child's pace - water confidence, regulation, and independence in the pool.

Climbing Activity

£75 / 60'
From 3 years+ Usually 60' Westway

Supported climbing that builds strength, focus, and confidence. Routes and support levels are matched to each child.

Physical Activity

£75 / 60'
From 12 years+ Usually 60' SwimFarm · Acton

Active movement sessions focused on coordination, stamina, and structure - adapted so every young person can take part safely.

Multi-Activity

£120 / 90'
From 3 years+ Usually 90' SwimFarm · Northolt

A longer block that combines activities in one visit (for example pool plus land-based work). Ideal when you want more variety in a single session.

Bespoke Programme

£125 / 60'
From 3 years+ Usually 60' (agreed with office) SwimFarm · Acton · Westway

A tailored programme built around your child's goals, support needs, and schedule - planned with you and the delivery team.

Day Centre

Day Centre

Quote individually
Mon-Fri · 11am-4pm SwimFarm

A weekday daytime programme with structured support through the day - table work, sensory / quiet time, gym, swimming, lunch and life skills, and community trips when staffing allows. Places are arranged with families through the office so we can match funding and support needs properly. Pricing is quoted individually (parent-funded, LA Direct Payments, or funder invoice).
