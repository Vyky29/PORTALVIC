1. Executive summary

CORRECTION: Care delivery is at the Day Centre (clients attend ~5 hours). Changing, medication if needed, and personal care happen on site — not as home visits. CQC Personal care usually requires care where the person lives; day centres are generally out of scope. Use this plan for NHS quality / commissioning conversations — do not file it as a domiciliary registration business case unless home care is added later.

clubSENsational LTD already delivers specialist autism / SEN support in London, including Day Centre packages commissioned by the NHS and private / referred clients, plus after-school and weekend activity programmes. Demand from commissioners (including the NHS) for continuous 1:1 adult support and personal care has led the directors to pursue CQC registration for Personal care (Path B — domiciliary care agency).

Aims of this plan: show local demand; describe a safe, small-scale autism/LD personal care model; demonstrate staffing, training and insurance readiness; and show financial viability alongside the existing business.

2. Market need & demand evidence

  • Existing NHS Day Centre packages for high-support autistic children / young people (portal examples: multi-package cohort ~£367k estimated for 2026–27).
  • Commissioner feedback that CQC registration would strengthen ability to commission / expand support where personal care is required.
  • Private and non-NHS families already using the service who need consistent adult supervision and personal care support.
  • Local shortage of autism-specialist domiciliary providers who can work 1:1 / 2:1 with complex sensory and communication needs.

Target customers

  • NHS and local authority packages for personal care at home / in the community
  • Families using personal budgets / direct payments
  • Continuity packages linked to existing Day Centre clients where home personal care is assessed as needed

3. Service model

In scope (CQC)

  • Usually outside CQC regulated activity while care stays centre-based
  • Service type: Day Centre / day support (not domiciliary home care)
  • Delivered at the Day Centre (~5h sessions) — on-site changing / meds as needed
  • Autism / LD specialist; PBS; accessible communication
  • 1:1 or 2:1 where risk-assessed

Out of scope / parallel

  • Day Centre (continues; not itself the regulated activity)
  • Swimming / climbing / Hub activities
  • No care home / overnight accommodation package
  • Housing remains separate from care agreements

Quality system: Statement of Purpose, Service User Guide, policies POL-001–021, portal ISPs / incidents / training, Hiscox EL/PL insurance.
