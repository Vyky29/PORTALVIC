1. Overall service model
????????????????????????????????

• Autism / SEN specialist day and activity support
• People travel to our venues for sessions (they do not receive domiciliary visits in their own homes)
• Day Centre sessions are typically around 5 hours (ordinarily Monday–Friday, about 11:00–16:00)
• Staffing is often 1:1 or 2:1 continuous adult support, as assessed
• Depending on need and the agreed plan, on-site support may include changing / toileting, personal hygiene after activities, and medication support (prompting or administration only if authorised and staff are competent)
• We do not provide overnight accommodation or care home services
• We do not currently provide personal care in people’s homes (a future domiciliary offer is possible, but is not our current model)

An NHS commissioner has suggested CQC registration would be helpful. Before we prepare any application, we need written confirmation whether this venue-based day / activity model is in or out of scope.

2. What we do at each venue
????????????????????????????????

A) clubSENsational Hub — W10 6RP
   Primary Day Centre base / multi-activity Hub.
   Typical activities:
   • Arrival and settling
   • Circle time and group belonging routines
   • Classroom / table work, fine-motor and vocational-style activities
   • Sensory regulation and (where needed) sensory-room support
   • Soft play / active play / movement and bespoke programmes
   • Life skills, snack and Hub regulation / relaxation before going home
   • On-site personal care as needed (e.g. changing / toileting; hygiene support)
   • Medication support only if written into the individual’s plan

B) SwimFarm Centre — W10 6RP
   Aquatic / pool venue linked to the Day Centre programme (same locality as Hub / Westway).
   Typical activities:
   • Aquatic familiarisation and SEN swimming / water-based sessions
   • Poolside continuous adult support (1:1 or 2:1 as assessed)
   • Changing-room transitions and post-swim shower / personal hygiene support on site
   • Return to Hub / Day Centre spaces for regulation, snack and further programme time

C) Westway Sports Centre — W10 6RP
   Sports / climbing and community activity venue (same postcode area as Hub / SwimFarm).
   Typical activities:
   • Climbing sessions and related sports / movement activities
   • Continuous adult support for autistic / SEN participants
   • Venue transitions and on-site personal care / changing support where needed during the session
   • May form part of a wider day programme alongside Hub and pool blocks

D) Acton Centre — W3 6LE
   Pool / activity venue (Everyone Active Acton).
   Typical activities:
   • SEN swimming and aquatic sessions
   • Poolside 1:1 / 2:1 support
   • Changing and post-swim hygiene support on site
   • Structured transitions and session feedback / recording

E) Northolt Centre — UB5 4AB
   Pool / activity venue (Everyone Active Northolt).
   Typical activities:
   • SEN swimming and aquatic sessions
   • Poolside 1:1 / 2:1 support
   • Changing and post-swim hygiene support on site
   • Structured transitions and session feedback / recording

3. Typical Day Centre flow (example)
????????????????????????????????

A common on-site sequence (varies by person and venue day) is:
arrival ? Hub / circle time / classroom or life-skills activities ? transition to pool or sports venue ? aquatic or climbing / sports block ? shower / changing / personal hygiene on site ? return to Hub for regulation / snack ? leave for home.

All of this happens at the venues above. Families collect participants at the end of the session. We do not deliver this personal care in the person’s home.
