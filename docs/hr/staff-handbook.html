2. Your contract (two main types)

Zero Hours

Activity Services

  • Swim, climb, fitness and related sessions outside the Day Centre
  • No guaranteed hours; accept only shifts you can do
  • Paid by the hour (inclusive rate)
  • Probation: usually 3 months
  • Notice: usually 2 weeks
  • You may work for other employers (no exclusivity)
Part-Time Salaried

Day Centre

  • Day Centre support only (typical paid hours 11:00-16:00 on your contracted days)
  • Fixed salary for contracted hours (includes holiday; same pay every month)
  • Probation: usually 6 months
  • Notice: usually 1 month
  • Does not cover evening Activity Services
Two contracts at once Some people hold both a Day Centre contract and a Zero Hours Activity Services contract. They run side by side. Use the Day Centre terms for Day Centre work, and the Zero Hours terms for Activity Services work. Each has its own pay and holiday rules (see section 4).

Regulated work needs a satisfactory enhanced DBS. Follow safeguarding and venue rules at all times.

4. Holiday (read this carefully)

Holiday year: 1 January - 31 December.

Zero Hours (Activity Services)

  • You are an irregular-hours worker.
  • Statutory holiday accrues at 12.07% of hours worked (subject to the statutory maximum).
  • That holiday pay is already inside your inclusive hourly rate and is shown separately on the payslip.
  • When you take time off, you are not paid holiday pay again (it was already paid with each shift).
  • You can still request time off so we can plan cover. Ask your Session Lead / manager in writing.

Part-Time Day Centre (salaried)

  • The annual salary includes statutory holiday pay. It is paid in 12 equal monthly instalments. There is no extra 12.07% on top.
  • You work your contracted hours during weeks the Day Centre is open (typically 43 weeks, as published each year).
  • Holiday is taken during published Day Centre closures. Leave is not normally approved while the service is open, except in exceptional circumstances agreed in writing (for example bereavement).
  • In closure weeks you still receive the same monthly salary — that is how holiday is paid.
  • Statutory family leave (maternity, paternity, adoption, shared parental, parental bereavement) is separate and is not blocked by this rule.

How to book leave

Day Centre part-time: your holiday is the published closure calendar. You do not book extra weeks off during open service, except exceptional cases agreed in writing. Check the calendar with your line manager.

Zero Hours:

  1. Check cover with your Session Lead / line manager.
  2. Request in writing (email your manager; copy admin@clubsensational.org if asked).
  3. Wait for written approval before booking travel. Time off is unpaid as holiday pay is already in the hourly rate.

Family leave (maternity, paternity, adoption, shared parental, parental bereavement): statutory rules if you qualify. Ask via the portal or admin@clubsensational.org.

Quick comparison Zero Hours: holiday pay = 12.07% inside the hourly rate → already paid with each shift.
Day Centre part-time: holiday pay = inside the annual salary → same monthly pay all year; time off = Day Centre closures.

5. Hours, venues & cancellations

  • Work where your contract / rota says (pools, climbing walls, Day Centre, community venues).
  • Zero Hours: accept only shifts you can complete. Employer cancel with ≥24 hours' notice → no pay for that shift. Cancel with <24 hours' notice → full shift pay (as in your contract).
  • Day Centre: work your contracted days and hours in weeks when the service is open. Holiday is taken during published closures. Salary is not reduced if a session is cancelled, provided you remain available for work.
  • Complete session / day feedback and records as part of the job.
  • Follow venue rules and Health & Safety instructions.

6. Sick

ContractWhat to do
Zero Hours Email a Company Director the night before if possible, or by 7:00 am on the day. Also tell your Session Lead so cover can be arranged.
Day Centre (salaried) Email a Company Director by 7:00 am on the day. Also tell your line manager.

Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) may apply if you qualify. There is no enhanced company sick pay unless a separate policy says so. Qualifying days for SSP on salaried contracts are usually Monday to Friday.

7. Safeguarding

Non-negotiable Follow the Safeguarding Policy. Report any concern to the Designated Safeguarding Lead immediately. Keep professional boundaries. No unauthorised photos or social media of participants.

Report incidents in the Staff Portal using the process for your role. If in doubt, tell your lead and admin@clubsensational.org at once.

8. Conduct, data & training

  • Keep participant and company information confidential during and after employment.
  • Use, look after and return kit, keys, ID and files when asked or when you leave.
  • Follow the Equipment & Uniform Policy.
  • Do not sign contracts or make commitments for the company unless authorised in writing.
  • Complete required training (safeguarding, induction, role training) when directed.

9. Problems at work

Grievance

Try an informal talk with your manager first. Formal process: put it in writing and follow the Grievance Policy (Policies Portal).

Disciplinary

Conduct or performance issues follow the Disciplinary Policy. You may be accompanied at formal meetings. Gross misconduct can mean dismissal without notice.

Serious wrongdoing: use the Whistleblowing Policy (Policies Portal).
