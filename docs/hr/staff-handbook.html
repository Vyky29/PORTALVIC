2. Your contract

Zero Hours

Activity Services

Swim / climb / sessions outside Day Centre. No guaranteed hours. Holiday pay rolled-up (12.07%) on payslip. Notice: usually 2 weeks.

Part-Time

Day Centre

Salaried. Typical paid hours 11:00–16:00 on your days. Paid holiday (not rolled-up). Notice: usually 1 month.

Some people have both contracts. Use the right one for the work that day. Job duties: Annex B of your contract.

Probation and DBS rules are in your contract. Regulated work needs a satisfactory enhanced DBS.

3. Pay & pension

  • Paid monthly (PAYE deductions as required).
  • Zero Hours: hours worked (+ any cancellation pay in your contract). Inclusive rates already include 12.07% holiday pay — itemised on the payslip; not paid again when you take leave.
  • Salaried: fixed salary while the contract runs.
  • Pension: NEST auto-enrolment if eligible (earnings from clubSENsational only). See Annex A of your contract.
  • Expenses: only if authorised — keep receipts; ask admin via portal / admin@clubsensational.org.

4. Hours & venues

  • Work where your contract / rota says (pools, climbing, Day Centre, community).
  • Zero Hours: accept only shifts you can do. Employer cancel ≥24h → no pay. Cancel <24h → full shift pay (per contract).
  • You may work for other employers on Zero Hours (no exclusivity).
  • Complete session feedback / records as part of the job.

5. Holiday

Holiday year: 1 Jan – 31 Dec.

  1. Check cover with your Session Lead / manager.
  2. Request in writing (email manager; copy admin@clubsensational.org if asked).
  3. Wait for written approval before booking travel.

Family leave (maternity, paternity, etc.): statutory rules if you qualify — ask via portal / admin@clubsensational.org.

6. Sick

YouNotify by
Zero HoursEmail a Company Director the night before, or by 7:00 am that day — and tell your lead for cover.
SalariedEmail a Company Director by 7:00 am that day — and tell your manager.

SSP may apply if you qualify. No enhanced company sick pay unless a policy says so.

7. Safeguarding

Non-negotiable Follow the Safeguarding Policy. Report concerns to the Designated Safeguarding Lead immediately. Professional boundaries always. No unauthorised photos / social media of participants.

Also follow Health & Safety and venue rules. Report incidents in the Portal (Staff incident / participant processes as directed).

8. Conduct & data

  • Keep participant and company information confidential — during and after employment.
  • Return kit, keys, ID and files when asked or when you leave.
  • Do not sign contracts for the company unless authorised in writing.
  • Complete required training (safeguarding, induction, role training).

9. Problems at work

Grievance

Try informal talk with your manager first. Formal: write it down → Grievance Policy (Policies Portal).

Disciplinary

Conduct/performance: Disciplinary Policy. You can be accompanied at formal meetings. Gross misconduct can mean dismissal.

Serious wrongdoing: Whistleblowing Policy (Policies Portal).

10. Leaving

Give written notice as in your contract (often 2 weeks Zero Hours; 1 month salaried). Return everything. Final pay and holiday pay as per statute / contract.

11. Policies you must sign

Open Staff Portal → Compliance → Policies and sign the policies for your role (Safeguarding, Disciplinary, Grievance, Data Protection, H&S, Equipment & Uniform, and others listed there). Re-sign when we update them.

No portal access? Email admin@clubsensational.org — we will send the documents and a sign-off form.
