How we work

Company policies: the standards every team member follows. This is not a step-by-step emergency guide. It tells you which policy area to open when you need the rules.

Policies vs procedures: policies set our expected standards (how we should work). Procedures tell you what to do when something happens. If you need the action map, open What to open. If you need the company rules, use this page and open the full policy in the Policies Portal.
