What to open

Use this when something happens or you are unsure which document to follow. It does not replace the procedure. It only points you to the right one in the Policies Portal.

How to use it: find your situation in the left column, open the named document in this portal, follow those steps, then record what happened in the Staff Portal. For fire, medical, missing person, behaviour crisis or pool emergency, you can also use the Emergency Guides on the right of the portal.

Urgent · keep people safe first

SituationOpen thisThen do this
Any emergencyFire, serious injury, immediate danger Emergency Response Principles Keep people safe. Call 999 if needed. Then use your venue emergency pack and the matching Emergency Guide.
Someone is missingParticipant not where they should be Missing Service User Procedure Start the search steps at once. Tell the lead / on-call. Report when the person is found or after escalation.
Pool emergencyDistress in water, rescue, pool first aid Aquatic Activity Procedure Also follow Emergency Response Principles. Use the Pool Emergency Guide for the first actions.
Home visit feels unsafeEnvironment risk, escalation, leave early Home Visit Emergency & Escalation Leave if needed. Call for support. Report as soon as you are safe.
Distressed behaviour or crisisEscalation, risk of harm, need for PBS Positive Behaviour Support (PBS) Stay calm and reduce triggers. Use Restrictive Practice only if needed and authorised. Report afterwards.

Concerns · report and follow process

SituationOpen thisThen do this
Safeguarding concernAbuse, neglect, unexplained injury, disclosure Safeguarding Reporting Procedure Do not investigate alone. Tell the DSL / manager. Also follow the Safeguarding Policy.
Incident, accident or near missSomeone hurt, almost hurt, property damage Incident Reporting Procedure Make the area safe. Get first aid if needed. Complete the incident report in the Staff Portal.
Complaint from family or commissionerVerbal or written complaint about the service Complaints Policy Listen, stay calm, do not argue. Pass it to the manager / complaints lead the same day.
